POPULAR CALL OF DUTY GUNS

Explore popular weapons and their roles in the game.

Assault rifle. A versatile choice for beginners and medium-range battles.

AK-47

Assault rifle known for its strong damage. Practice controlling recoil.

MP5

Submachine gun suited to close-range encounters and fast movement.

SNIPER RIFLE

Designed for long-range engagements. Patience and accuracy are important.

LEARN HOW TO PLAY

New to Call of Duty? Start here!

01. Choose Your Game

Choose your preferred version, such as Call of Duty Mobile, Warzone or another Call of Duty title.

02. Understand The Controls

Learn how to move, aim, shoot, reload, crouch and change weapons.

03. Choose Your Weapons

Try assault rifles, sniper rifles and submachine guns. Find a weapon that suits your playing style.

04. Practise Your Aim

Use practice modes to improve accuracy, reaction time and movement.

05. Play With A Team

Communicate with teammates, support one another and develop strategies.

06. Become A Champion

Keep practising, learn from mistakes and improve with every match!
